## Forensic Digital Humanities Infrastructure & Relational Research Platform

This directory contains the production-ready presentation deliverables for **Presentation 01**, prepared for Professor Mairaj Syed, academic colleagues, digital humanities researchers, and institutional research sponsors/funders.

### Delivery Options

#### Option A: Dedicated Next.js Interactive Route (Integrated)
- **URL:** `http://localhost:3000/presentation`
- **Source File:** `mizan-web/frontend/src/app/presentation/page.tsx`
- **Features:** 
  - Integrated with Next.js 15 client
  - Ambient Islamic background geometry and balance motifs
  - Direct live links to Mīzān workbench tools (`/books`, `/isnad`, `/hadith-visualizer`, `/ask`)
  - Keyboard controls (`←`, `→`, `Space`, `N` for Speaker Notes, `O` for Slide Grid, `F` for Fullscreen)
  - Real-time elapsed presentation stopwatch

#### Option B: Standalone Zero-Dependency Offline Deck (Fail-Safe)
- **Path:** `presentation/presentation_01/index.html`
- **Features:**
  - 100% self-contained HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript
  - No internet connection required
  - No Python, Node.js, or local servers required
  - Double-click to launch in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge
  - Complete with all 15 slides, speaker notes drawer, overview grid, and keyboard shortcuts

---

### Keyboard Shortcuts
- `→` / `Space` / `Page Down`: Advance to next slide
- `←` / `Page Up`: Return to previous slide
- `Home`: Jump to Slide 01 (Title Folio)
- `End`: Jump to Slide 15 (Closing Folio)
- `N`: Toggle Speaker Notes drawer
- `O`: Toggle 15-Slide Overview Grid
- `F`: Toggle Fullscreen mode
- `Esc`: Close open drawers/modals
