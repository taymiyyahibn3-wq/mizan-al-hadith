## 1. Compliance Principles & Fair Use Foundations

Mīzān al-Ḥadīth is an academic digital humanities platform committed to transparent copyright compliance, scholarly attribution, and ethical digital custody:
1. **No False Licensing Claims:** No dataset or image is claimed as "Public Domain" or "Creative Commons" without verified documentary evidence.
2. **Clear Attribution:** All external contributions, photographic scans, translations, and software libraries receive explicit attribution with author and license details.
3. **Integrity of Islamic Heritage:** Classical Arabic texts composed over a millennium ago are recognized as the universal cultural heritage of humanity, preserved for study and computational research.
